Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Safari Ember collects when you use this website, book a room, pay a deposit or get in touch with us; why we collect it; who we share it with; how long we keep it; and the rights you have over it. We handle your information in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Who we are

Safari Ember is a lodge at 13 Bogoria Lane, Thika, Kenya. We decide how and why your personal information is used, which makes us the data controller for it. For anything about your information, email stay@safariember.com or call +254 705 439 631.

2. What we collect

When you book

  • Your first and last name, email address and phone number.
  • Your stay: room, dates, number of guests, and any requests you add (a late arrival or an anniversary, for example).
  • Your booking reference and its status (held, confirmed or cancelled), with the times those changed.

When you pay a deposit

  • For M-Pesa: the phone number we send the payment prompt to, the amount, and the M-Pesa receipt number.
  • For cards: the amount, the payment reference, and the card type with its last four digits. We never see or store your full card number, expiry date or security code. These go straight to our card processor (see Payments).

When you get in touch

  • Your name, email address and the message you write in the enquiry form, or whatever you include when you call, email or message us on WhatsApp.

When you browse

  • Like any website, our servers receive your IP address and basic technical details (browser type, the page requested) with every visit. We use these to keep the site running and to stop abuse, such as repeated automated booking attempts.

We don't ask for sensitive information such as health details. If you choose to tell us something like a dietary need or an accessibility request, we use it only to prepare for your stay.

3. How we use it, and why we may

  • To take and manage your booking: holding your room, confirming it, sending your booking details and preparing for your arrival. We need this information to carry out our agreement with you.
  • To take your deposit and to match each payment to the right booking, including handling refunds.
  • To answer your enquiries and to contact you about your stay.
  • To keep records that Kenyan law requires, such as tax and accounting records.
  • To protect the site and our guests from fraud and misuse, which is in our legitimate interest and yours.

We do not sell your information, and we do not send you marketing unless you have asked us to. You can stop it at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to do its job for us:

  • Safaricom, to send the M-Pesa payment prompt to your phone and confirm the payment.
  • Paystack, to process card payments securely.
  • Our hosting and database providers, which run the website and store booking records on our behalf, under agreements that require them to keep the data secure and use it only for us.
  • Google, if you view the map in the Location section (see Cookies).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store data outside Kenya. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the Data Protection Act requires for transfers abroad.

5. Payments

Deposits are paid through M-Pesa or by card through Paystack. Card details are entered on Paystack's secure checkout, not on our site, and are handled under Paystack's own security standards and privacy policy. M-Pesa payments are handled by Safaricom under its own terms. We receive only confirmation of each payment and the details listed in What we collect.

6. Cookies

Our own pages don't set cookies and don't use advertising or tracking tools. Two third-party features may set their own cookies:

  • The Google Map in the Location section loads from Google as you scroll near it, and Google may set cookies when it does. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Paystack's checkout may set cookies needed to complete and secure a card payment.

Our pages also load their fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

7. How long we keep it

  • Unpaid holds expire after 15 minutes. The room is released, but the record of the attempt is kept so that any late payment can be traced and refunded.
  • Bookings and payments are kept for as long as Kenyan tax and accounting law requires, then deleted.
  • Enquiries are kept for as long as we need to answer them and handle any stay that follows, then deleted.
  • Server logs are kept briefly for security and then deleted.

8. Keeping it safe

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Booking pages are reached through private links that only work for your booking. Access to booking records is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be told how your information is used, as this policy does;
  • see the information we hold about you;
  • have anything inaccurate corrected;
  • have your information deleted, unless we must keep it by law;
  • object to how we use it, or ask us to limit that use; and
  • receive your information in a common format, or have it sent to someone else.

To use any of these rights, email stay@safariember.com. We will reply within the time the law sets. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we'll let guests with upcoming stays know. Questions are always welcome at stay@safariember.com.
