Beyond the Hotel

Signature Attractions

Wildlife

Nairobi National Park

The only wildlife park in the world set against a city skyline. Lions, rhinos, buffalo, and hundreds of bird species roam freely. All within minutes of your suite at Safari Ember. A safari experience like no other, with the Nairobi skyline as your backdrop.

Book Safari

Conservation

Giraffe Centre

A sanctuary for the endangered Rothschild giraffe, the Giraffe Centre offers a rare and intimate encounter with one of Africa's most magnificent creatures. Feed them by hand, learn their stories, and leave with a memory that lingers long after your stay.

Book Safari

Heritage

Karen Blixen Museum

The former home of the celebrated author of Out of Africa, this beautifully preserved colonial farmhouse sits amid lush gardens at the foot of the Ngong Hills. A window into Kenya's storied past and the romantic era that inspired one of literature's greatest memoirs.

Book Safari
