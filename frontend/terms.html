1. About us

The site is run by Safari Ember, 13 Bogoria Lane, Thika, Kenya. You can reach us at stay@safariember.com or +254 705 439 631.

2. Using the site

You're welcome to browse the site and book stays for yourself and the people travelling with you. Please don't misuse it: no automated booking or scraping, no attempts to interfere with its security, and no bookings made in someone else's name without their permission. We may limit or block access where we see misuse.

We work to keep room descriptions, photos and prices accurate, but rooms can vary slightly from their pictures, and features may occasionally change.

3. Making a booking

  • When you choose a room and dates and enter your details, we hold the room for 15 minutes while you pay the deposit. If the deposit isn't paid in that time, the hold ends and the room is released to other guests.
  • Your booking is confirmed only when your deposit is received. You'll see the confirmation on your booking page, with your booking reference (for example SE-7K3QXM).
  • Please make sure your name, email and phone number are correct. We use them to confirm your stay and to contact you about it.
  • Prices are shown in Kenya shillings (KES) and include the rates for the nights you choose. Seasonal rates apply automatically to the dates they cover.

4. Deposits and payment

  • A deposit of 30% of the total stay is paid online to confirm your booking. The balance is paid at the lodge.
  • You can pay the deposit by M-Pesa (we send a payment prompt to your phone) or by card through Paystack's secure checkout. We never see your full card details.
  • A payment counts as received once we have confirmed it directly with Safaricom or Paystack.

5. Late or unusual payments

  • If your deposit arrives after the 15-minute hold has ended, we'll still confirm your booking if the room is still free. If someone else has booked it in the meantime, we'll refund your payment in full.
  • If a payment arrives for a different amount or currency than your deposit, we'll pause the booking and contact you to put it right before confirming anything.

6. Changes and cancellations

  • Changes: to change your dates or room, contact us. We'll do our best to help, depending on availability, and any difference in price is settled with your balance.
  • Cancelling 14 days or more before arrival: your deposit is refunded in full.
  • Cancelling less than 14 days before arrival, or not arriving: the deposit is kept.
  • Refunds go back to the M-Pesa number or card the deposit came from, normally within 14 days.
  • If we ever have to cancel your stay, for example because of an emergency at the lodge, we'll refund everything you've paid.

7. During your stay

  • Check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out is by 11:00 am. Earlier or later times can often be arranged; just ask.
  • Each room has a maximum number of guests, shown when you book.
  • Please treat the lodge, its staff and other guests with respect. We may charge for damage beyond normal wear.
  • Activities such as game drives and visits to the Giraffe Centre may be run with partners and may have their own terms, fees and safety rules.
